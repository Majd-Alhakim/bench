Your email, and nothing else

This page covers the eucas.app website and its waitlist. The Eucas app gets its own privacy note before the first build ships.

What we keep

  • Your email address.
  • Your answers, if you give them: which agents you use, how many sessions you run at once, and which computer.
  • Which button you came from (Free or Pro), and a source tag such as "x" or "reddit" if the link you followed had one.
  • When you joined and when you confirmed, and the exact consent line you agreed to.

We don't keep your IP address, your browser details or your name.

What we use it for

Three emails at most: a link to confirm, the first Windows build, and Pro when it opens. Your answers decide what gets built first. We never sell, rent or share the list, and we never add you to anything else.

Where it lives

In a Cloudflare D1 database that only the founder can open. The confirm email is sent through Resend. Cloudflare checks the form for bots with Turnstile, which doesn't set tracking cookies.

Counting visits

We count page views with Cloudflare Web Analytics. It sets no cookies and doesn't follow you across sites, so there is no cookie banner.

Leaving

Every email has a link that deletes your email and answers in one click. Or write to hello@eucas.app and we'll delete them by hand, or send you a copy of everything we hold about you.

If you never confirm, your email is deleted after 30 days.
