# Strategy update: after the teardowns (2026-10-22)

Inputs: the [teardowns](teardowns/README.md) of Grok Bot, OpenAI Dots and Claude Code, plus the [competitive brief](competitive-brief.md). Status: **PM proposal**. Items marked ⚑ change requirements and need client approval (they go into CR-002/CR-003, not straight into the BRD).

## 1. What changed in our understanding
| We believed | Evidence now shows | So |
|---|---|---|
| Grok Bot's problem is a weak model | It routes to strong models (Claude, Grok, Gemini) but **hides the choice and the cost**. Its bills explode because of **unbounded context per bot** | Our wedge isn't "Claude is smarter". It is **context and cost discipline you can see** |
| Competitors have no shared memory | Grok Bot has shared user memory, Dots syncs ChatGPT memory, and **neither can be viewed or edited** | Our wedge is **memory you can see, edit and trace** (Bench Memory) |
| Claude Code teams could be our engine | **Agent teams don't run through the SDK**, they're experimental, and they lose state | Bench owns orchestration. **Persistence is our moat** |
| Avatars are decoration | Dots uses character avatars to **tell agents apart and show status**; Grok Bot uses names, labels and badges | Avatars are a **status and identity system**, not art |
| Safety means approvals | Dots: a 4-level approval ladder, an uneditable reviewer model, read-only background work enforced in code | Our approval model needs levels plus an independent check |

## 2. Positioning (sharpened)
> **Bench is your delivery department on your own machine: a team of Claude agents with their own models, tools and memory, where you can see every step, every token and every memory, and nothing runs away with your usage.**

Three proof points everyone else lacks:
1. **Context and cost you can see**: a meter per agent, a checkpoint and fresh-session restart, cost per message, a weekly view per agent. Grok Bot's #1 complaint is our #1 feature.
2. **Memory you can open**: Markdown, Obsidian-compatible, editable, with a record of who wrote what.
3. **A real team that persists**: an org tree per project, leads, loans and a durable task board. That's what Claude Code teams and Dots don't offer today.
