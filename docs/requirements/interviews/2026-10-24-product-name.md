# Product name: Euca (2026-10-24)

Closes D-UI5 (the name). This came out of a brainstorm on 2026-10-23 and 2026-10-24. "Bench" stays the codename in the repo until the rename task (below).

## Decision
- **Name:** **Euca**, pronounced "you-ka". It's written in lowercase in the lockup (`euca`).
- **The characters:** the agents are **Eucas** ("you-kuz"), the way a group of Smurfs is Smurfs. Atlas is your first Euca.
- **Origin:** from *eucatastrophe*, Tolkien's word for the sudden turn in a story that saves the hero from doom and ends happily.
- **Client's words:** *"whenever a user is stuck with an Idea, or facing a challenge in building an app, a Euca is the exact character who pops up to save the day and turn a disaster into a win"*. Recorded: *"Yes. Record Euca as the product name (characters are Eucas), with the risks noted, and update the brand docs."*

## Why it fits
- **The why:** ideas die half-built and keep coming back. The client wants to see them through, in their own control, and feel proud to share them. A eucatastrophe is that turn from doom to a happy ending.
- **The client's naming rules:** one word, easy to say and remember, a meaning close to the why, not generic, and free by the realistic bar (no one in AI, agents or app building uses it, no well-known brand, a usable domain). It also has to carry a slogan and a campaign.
- **Slogan to test:** *Every idea deserves a happy ending.* Content format: people share their own "eucatastrophe", the moment a stuck project turned around.
- **Lore rule (PM):** **you** stay the hero. Eucas are the crew that turns your story around while you decide, test and accept. They don't take over (BR-86, CR-008).

## Freedom check (2026-10-24, web search + DNS; not a legal clearance)
- No app, startup or brand named Euca or Eucas was found.
- **Domains:** `euca.ai`, `eucas.app`, `eucas.ai` and `geteuca.com` have no DNS, so they may be available. `euca.com` is registered, `euca.app` is a password-protected Framer site, and `euca.dev` is a photographer's portfolio.
- **npm:** `euca` and `eucas` are free.

## Risks (accepted by the client)
| Risk | Severity | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|
| **Euka AI** (San Francisco, 2024; TikTok Shop affiliate marketing) sounds the same | Medium | Different spelling and field. Always write "Euca" with its story. Check it in the trademark search |
| **Eucalyptus** (Australian digital-health company, part of Hims & Hers, often "Euc"); the word reads like the plant | Low – Medium | The Tolkien origin story leads every first touch; no plant imagery |
| Pronunciation on first read | Low | Onboarding and the site say "you-ka" once; the sound mark can say it |
| Old dev tooling: `euca2ools`, the Eucalyptus cloud CLI | Low | Legacy and niche; noted only |
