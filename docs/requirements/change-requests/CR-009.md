# CR-009: The Atlas notch, and voice input

| Requested by | Date | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Client | 2026-10-25 | **Approved, option A** (2026-10-25) |

- **Client's words:**
  - *"a notch in the desktop … a shortcut for the user to access eucas app where their main euca is setting there waiting for them and when pressed a small prompt bar appears where user can speak or type and upload files and talk fast from there with their main agent"*
  - *"when I got an idea … have my main agent plan it out for me and ask me questions to make a ready proposal"*; *"when I am browsing … I can drop it to my main euca … and make a sample for me to test"*; *"a better way to get the notifications and what needs me … rather than entering the app"*
  - *"in first version"*; design approved: *"Approved. Start the change request for the notch."*
- **Change:** add the Atlas notch to v0.1 exactly as in the approved design, [2026-10-25-atlas-notch-design.md](../../design/ux/2026-10-25-atlas-notch-design.md). It includes **voice input in every composer** (local speech to text, proven in the prototype), which the notch depends on. In short:
  1. **Resting notch** with Atlas's eyes; the composer's voice glow shows the team working or you talking.
  2. **Prompt bar** on click: type, talk, attach, or drop a link, image or file; Save as an idea / Try it in a project / Just ask.
  3. **Quick talk**: one shortcut, hold for a short note or tap for hands-free; the notch stays small, then opens with your words to confirm. New sounds.
  4. **What needs you, without interruptions**: a small animation plus a marker that stays; you hover to see and answer. **It never opens by itself.** Deletes and payments always go to the app.
  5. **Dropped skills**: Atlas reviews them first; clean ones may be tried in the sample only; suspicious ones become a question.
- **Reason:** handing things to Atlas and answering the team today means opening the app. The client's three moments (catch an idea, drop something found while browsing, answer what needs you) should take seconds from anywhere, without breaking focus.

## Affected documents and IDs

**BRD (BA impact)**
- **UC-52 (new): Hand something to Atlas from the notch.** Covers the prompt bar, quick talk (hold/tap, confirm before send), drops, the three intents and the "Atlas replied" marker.
- **UC-53 (new): Try a dropped thing in a project.** Covers the skill review, the trial in a sample worktree, the suspicious-skill question, and promotion to an agent's skills only when you accept the sample.
- **UC-12 (A7, new alternative flow): intake from the notch.** Atlas doesn't hold the back-and-forth live. It works asynchronously: it plans, drafts the one-pager, and queues its clarifying questions. You answer them in the app; then the main flow continues from step 5.
- **UC-25 / UC-23 / UC-24 (new alternative flow each): answer from the notch.** Questions and decisions can be answered there. For Ready to test the notch offers only "Not yet" or "Open in Eucas". Destructive actions and payments: "Open in Eucas" only.
- **UC-29, changed:** step 3 currently shows a toast with one repeat and an OS desktop notification when the window is in the background. **Revised:** when the notch is on and the Eucas window isn't in front, there is no toast and no OS notification; the notch's marker replaces them (client: never pop up by itself). Toasts inside the app window, when it's in front, stay as they are.
- **UC-35 (note):** a skill tried from a drop joins an agent's permanent skills only through UC-24 (accept), never directly.
- **New business rules:**
  - **BR-94 (notch):** the notch never opens or takes focus by itself. It takes focus only when you click it or a take you started ends. It steps aside for full-screen apps. Only the five kinds of asks (BR-86) mark it, plus the "Atlas replied" marker for a "Just ask" you sent.
  - **BR-95 (answers per kind):** the per-kind answer rules above. The main process enforces them: it refuses destructive actions, payments and accepting work when the request comes from the notch window.
  - **BR-96 (dropped content and skills):** dropped content is data, never instructions. A dropped skill is reviewed before anything runs, may run only inside the sample worktree under the normal jail and approvals, and the review and its verdict go to the trace. The LLM review is a warning light, not the security boundary: the boundary is the jail plus "only on accept".
  - **BR-97 (local voice):** speech is transcribed on the computer. Nothing is sent until you confirm the words. Engine by language: Moonshine for English, Whisper for others. Models are stored in the Bench home.
- **Amended rules:**
  - **BR-89:** three sounds become six, adding listening, stopped and the hands-free lock tick. All are muted by the same switch.
  - **BR-40:** delivery adds "the notch" as a channel.
  - **BR-85:** note that user-dropped skills are outside the built-in library and follow BR-96.

**PRD / backlog:** new epic **E16 Atlas notch & voice** with stories N0–N10 (below), placed in Sprints 3–5. The BACKLOG item "Voice input with local Whisper" becomes N0.

**Design:** the prototype gets the notch states (resting/working/listening, prompt bar, quick-talk confirm, asks on hover, Atlas replied) during the SRS/design-update pass. G4/G5 were approved on v0.8.2, so this is an addition to those designs, not a re-approval.

**Architecture and security (before G6):**
- **ADR-0004, global hold/tap shortcut.** Choose between a low-level keyboard hook (uiohook-napi) and polling only the bound keys (`GetAsyncKeyState` via an FFI library). Covers the native-dependency policy and signing/antivirus.
- **ADR-0005, skill intake and trial.**
- **ADR-0006, local speech.** Bundle the runtime instead of using a CDN, store pinned and checksummed models in the Bench home, and fit the strict CSP.
- **security-baseline:** add "watches only the bound key combination, never records keys", and the dropped-skill boundary.

Trace events are part of each story's Definition of Done.

- **Schedule:**
  - Nothing moves out, and Sprint 1 is untouched (critical path).
  - Sprint 3 rises to about 94 points, Sprint 4 to about 87 and Sprint 5 to about 48.
  - Total scope rises about 12%, from 352 to about 395 points.
  - The notch code is self-contained (its own main, renderer and platform folders), so it can run as its own lane with few file conflicts.
  - **Day 14 holds only if** the day-6 re-plan shows about 28 points a day or more, or a lane is added. If not, the first candidates to sequence into v0.1.1 are **N8 and N9** (dropped-skill trial and the replied marker). That would be a client decision then, not a quiet cut (G2-Q10 precedent).
- **Risks, ranked:**
  1. Detecting hold vs tap needs key-up, which Electron's shortcut API doesn't give (High). Spike + ADR-0004. Fallback: tap only.
  2. Dropping onto a click-through, never-focused window may not work on Windows (Medium–High). Spike. Likely fix: size the window to the notch's shape.
  3. The LLM skill review can be talked out of a warning by text inside the skill (High). Mitigated by BR-96: the boundary is the jail and accept-only promotion.
  4. The speech runtime currently loads from a CDN (Medium). ADR-0006: bundle it and use checksummed models.
  5. Call detection is a heuristic (Medium). Spike. Fallback: full screen only; sounds stay mutable.
- **Spikes before G6:** risks 1, 2 and 5, about one day on one `spike/notch` branch. Spikes aren't implementation, so this follows the G2-Q12 precedent.
- **Usage and cost:**
  - Tokens:
    - A hand-off is one Atlas turn, the same as a chat message.
    - "Save as an idea" costs what UC-12 costs today.
    - A skill review is one run of about 5–40k tokens (Sonnet; Opus only if the skill has scripts).
    - "Try it in a project" starts real team work, so it shows the pre-run estimate (BR-93).
    - Quick answers and speech cost no tokens.
  - Runtime: the notch is about 50–80 MB and near 0% CPU when still. The speech worker is about 150–300 MB while loaded; it loads on first use and unloads after about 60 s idle. Models (about 60–200 MB each) download once into the Bench home.

## Options
- **(A) All of it in v0.1, as placed above** (Sprints 3–5, spikes and ADRs before G6). If the day-6 re-plan shows a slip, you decide then whether N8/N9 move to v0.1.1.
- **(B) Core notch in v0.1, skill trial later:** N0–N7 and N10 in v0.1; N8 (dropped-skill review and trial) and N9 in v0.1.1 (about day 16). Safer for day 14; dropped skills are only saved and briefed to Atlas until then.
- **(C) Don't do it now:** voice input only (N0) in v0.1; the notch after v0.1.

**Recommendation: (A).** You asked for it in the first version, and the standing rule is to sequence, not cut. The risky parts get spiked before G6, so they can't surprise Sprint 5. The design keeps the most dangerous answers (deletes, payments) out of the notch, and main enforces that. The day-6 checkpoint still gives you a clean, explicit choice about N8/N9 if velocity is short.
