# CR-012: Everyone signs in with a Eucas account

| Requested by | Date | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Client | 2026-10-26 | **Approved** (*"Approve CR-012"*, 2026-10-26, with the privacy line and the email asked after the retro); **applied in BRD v1.5 / PRD v0.6** |

## Client's words

- On which budget a Free user's rule drafts count against: *"everyone have to create an account"*.

## Change

**1. Every install signs in to a Eucas account in setup**, Free included. The account is an **email address plus a one-time code**: no password, nothing for Eucas to store or leak. Your Claude login is untouched; Eucas never sees or handles it (ADR-0002).

**2. The account holds what's per person:**
- the supervisor budget (trial, monthly taste, Pro) and the prompted-rule drafts that count against it;
- the trial entitlement (one per account), which replaces "one trial per verified email";
- the Pro licence and the founders' $15 for life, which belong to the person (BR-117 (8));
- the waitlist and mailing list.

**3. The account holds nothing about your work.** Rules, the ledger, receipts and "What Pip knows" stay on the PC. The server keeps the email, the plan, the budget used ~~and a device count~~, and nothing else. **Revised 2026-10-26:** no device count is stored; the server also keeps the product-news choice, and after Delete account only a keyed hash of the email and a "trial used" flag.

**4. Offline still works once you're signed in.** The guard, limits, tripwire and rules never need the account; only model calls (drafting, supervisor) and the licence check do. If the server is down, Pip says so and asks you instead.

**5. ~~Sign-in is mandatory~~ (founder, 2026-10-26):** *"don't allow to access without sign in"*. There is no "Sign in later". ~~Offline at the sign-in step, setup waits: "You need to be online to sign in. Pip will start once you're connected." with Retry and Back. Signed out (after Sign out or Delete account), the panel shows only sign-in,~~ while the guard and the locked limits keep protecting the PC (confirmed, *"yes"*); the supervisor, drafting, Usage and Rules editing need ~~sign-in~~ an account. **Partly reversed 2026-10-26; see the revision note below.**

## Revision note (2026-10-26): setup can finish without an account

Founder: *"we make people have a small taste without sign up and then they have to create account or buy pro"*. Reason: Show HN and the awesome-claude-code list exclude sign-up-gated tools. Later the same day: *"Let the no account also have to try the supervisor also but like small number of usage"*.

- **Setup can finish without an account.** The sign-in step gains a secondary **Continue without an account** (online or offline); that path runs Choose your rules (one own rule), What is sent and a no-account "Pip is watching". This partly reverses change 1 ("every install signs in") and change 5 above.
- **No account = everything a Free account sees, minus memory** (founder, *"Everything a free account sees but with limited supervisour usage and no memory and 1 custom role"*): the full notch and panel (Usage, Rules), receipts and the ledger; no learns you, What Pip knows or learned suggestions; **exactly one own rule** ("Create an account for more rules"); a supervisor taste of **10 decisions per install**, tracked by a random install ID (approved, *"Yes install ID"*) and a signed install token on anonymous routes. Without an account the server keeps only the install ID and the budget used. An account adds memory, more rules, the 14-day trial (which starts **when the account is created**) and the monthly taste; Pro needs an account.
- **Counts** (*"Show counts always, but as meter not dollars or money that what i said before"*): never dollars or money; counts as a meter are fine.
- **Never nags:** one quiet invite where the user already looks; no timed pop-ups, reminders, badges or countdowns. When the taste runs out, Pip asks you, plus one line, once: "Sign up for 14 more days of the supervisor".
- Signing out or deleting the account returns to the no-account state, not a sign-in-only panel.
- **Answered the same day** (BRD §10 #66 – #68): the trial starts at account creation; the no-account surface is everything Free sees, minus memory, with one own rule; the taste is 10 per install.
- Applied in BRD v1.5 Update 5 (new BR-130, UC-54 A18, UC-65 A11, UC-66 A8), PRD v0.6 (28.5 – 28.8, §11.1 #30 – #32) and SRS revision 1.1, approved as a revision note (*"Yes update SRS"*; FR-190 – FR-200). **Why this still holds for CR-012's reasons:** the budget, the trial, Pro and the founders' price stay per account; the per-install taste is small enough that a reinstall costs almost nothing.

## Why

One account answers the open budget question for Free users (the per-IP rate limit was a stopgap), stops trial abuse more cleanly than email-plus-device binding, makes "for life" enforceable per person, and gives the mailing list a real address. It costs one extra setup step and an email on our server.
