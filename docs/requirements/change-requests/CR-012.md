# CR-012: Everyone signs in with a Eucas account

| Requested by | Date | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Client | 2026-10-26 | **Approved** (*"Approve CR-012"*, 2026-10-26, with the privacy line and the email asked after the retro); **applied in BRD v1.5 / PRD v0.6** |

## Client's words

- On which budget a Free user's rule drafts count against: *"everyone have to create an account"*.

## Change

**1. Every install signs in to a Eucas account in setup**, Free included. The account is an **email address plus a one-time code**: no password, nothing for Eucas to store or leak. Your Claude login is untouched; Eucas never sees or handles it (ADR-0002).

**2. The account holds what's per person:**
- the supervisor budget (trial, monthly taste, Pro) and the prompted-rule drafts that count against it;
- the trial entitlement (one per account), which replaces "one trial per verified email";
- the Pro licence and the founders' $15 for life, which belong to the person (BR-117 (8));
- the waitlist and mailing list.

**3. The account holds nothing about your work.** Rules, the ledger, receipts and "What Pip knows" stay on the PC. The server keeps the email, the plan, the budget used ~~and a device count~~, and nothing else. **Revised 2026-10-26:** no device count is stored; the server also keeps the product-news choice, and after Delete account only a keyed hash of the email and a "trial used" flag.

**4. Offline still works once you're signed in.** The guard, limits, tripwire and rules never need the account; only model calls (drafting, supervisor) and the licence check do. If the server is down, Pip says so and asks you instead.

**5. Sign-in is mandatory (founder, 2026-10-26):** *"don't allow to access without sign in"*. There is no "Sign in later". Offline at the sign-in step, setup waits: "You need to be online to sign in. Pip will start once you're connected." with Retry and Back. Signed out (after Sign out or Delete account), the panel shows only sign-in, while the guard and the locked limits keep protecting the PC (confirmed, *"yes"*); the supervisor, drafting, Usage and Rules editing need sign-in.

## Why

One account answers the open budget question for Free users (the per-IP rate limit was a stopgap), stops trial abuse more cleanly than email-plus-device binding, makes "for life" enforceable per person, and gives the mailing list a real address. It costs one extra setup step and an email on our server.
