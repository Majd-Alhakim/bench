# CR-012: Everyone signs in with a Eucas account

| Requested by | Date | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Client | 2026-10-26 | **Proposed** (the decision is the client's; this impact analysis waits for approval before it's applied) |

## Client's words

- On which budget a Free user's rule drafts count against: *"everyone have to create an account"*.

## Change

**1. Every install signs in to a Eucas account in setup**, Free included. The account is an **email address plus a one-time code**: no password, nothing for Eucas to store or leak. Your Claude login is untouched; Eucas never sees or handles it (ADR-0002).

**2. The account holds what's per person:**
- the supervisor budget (trial, monthly taste, Pro) and the prompted-rule drafts that count against it;
- the trial entitlement (one per account), which replaces "one trial per verified email";
- the Pro licence and the founders' $15 for life, which belong to the person (BR-117 (8));
- the waitlist and mailing list.

**3. The account holds nothing about your work.** Rules, the ledger, receipts and "What Pip knows" stay on the PC. The server keeps the email, the plan, the budget used and a device count, and nothing else.

**4. Offline still works.** The guard, limits, tripwire and rules never need the account; only model calls (drafting, supervisor) and the licence check do. If the server is down, Pip says so and asks you instead.

## Why

One account answers the open budget question for Free users (the per-IP rate limit was a stopgap), stops trial abuse more cleanly than email-plus-device binding, makes "for life" enforceable per person, and gives the mailing list a real address. It costs one extra setup step and an email on our server.

## Impact

| Area | Change |
|---|---|
| **Setup (UC-54)** | A new step after "Make your Euca": email → 6-digit code. Setup goes from about 4:30 to about 5:00. The approved design D-3 ("trial email optional") is replaced: the email is now required. "No account needed" is removed from UC-54 and the onboarding copy |
| **Privacy (BR-115, SECURITY-PRIVACY)** | The server stores an email. The privacy line becomes: "Nothing about your work leaves this PC unless you turn it on. Your account holds only your email and your plan." The **copy needs your approval** |
| **Server (ADR-0011, ADR-0012)** | New routes: `/v1/auth/start` (sends the code), `/v1/auth/verify` (returns a short-lived signed token). D1 table: email, plan, budget used, device count. Email sending through Cloudflare Email Service or Resend, about $0 at our volume. All model routes require the token; the per-IP limit stays only as abuse protection |
| **Design (#67)** | One new setup screen (email, code, resend, wrong code, offline); Settings → Account (email, plan, sign out, delete account) |
| **Build** | PM's rough guess **+2 days** (auth routes, email, the setup step, the account page, tests), **to be replaced by the tech lead's estimate** when applied |
| **Delete account** | Settings → Delete account removes the server record; local data stays until you uninstall. This is the user's own action on their own data, not an agent delete (CR-008) |
| **Cost** | Email: free tier. D1: free tier at our scale. No change to the $100/month ceiling |

## Open points for the client

1. **Copy:** approve the new privacy line above.
2. **Sign-in timing:** setup asks for the email **before** the retro (so a Free user's first model call is covered), or **after** it (so you see value first). Recommended: **after** the retro, before choosing rules, since drafting is the first step that needs the server.
