        Bench design system · direction v0.1 · light mode (dark is Phase 5b)

        A white room, one blue switch, and a family of characters.

        Apple carries the structure. The agents carry the colour.

        Colour

        60 % neutral canvas, 30 % white surfaces and text, 10 % accent. The accent is Apple Blue, used only for the single filled action on a screen and for focus. Agent hues live only in avatars, name tags, mention chips, the client's bubble tint, a faint wash behind the agent's chat, and the agent's thinking dots.

        Neutrals and action

        Feedback (always paired with a word)

        Agent palette: 10 soft hues, never on buttons

        HueAvatarBase · light · deepName tag (ink on white)Client bubble (Carbon on tint)Mention chip (ink on tint)Wash

        Type

        One family (Inter, or SF on macOS) plus a monospace for code. Bench body is 15 px, not Apple's 17 px, because this is a dense desktop work app. Tracking follows Apple's negative curve.

        Voice

        Agents write short, lowercase, concrete lines ("done. 41 lines removed."). The interface and system lines use sentence case. Run summaries are scannable checklists: key → result · detail.

        Space, shape, elevation

        A 4-px scale. Panels pad 16 or 24, elements sit 12 apart, sections 40 to 64. Two radii plus one exception. No shadows on UI; depth comes from surface shifts (Frost → White) and 1-px hairlines.

        Radius

        980 px · buttons, tags, chips
        8 px · cards, inputs, panes
        18 px · chat bubbles (bend)

        Elevation

        None. The only shadow in the product is the soft ground shadow under a hero avatar, which counts as imagery (Apple allows shadow on product imagery only).

        Status

        One vocabulary, always as a word. The corner badge is a second cue: one glyph per state, readable at 24 px. Filled badges need attention. Only Thinking and Working move, and they stop under reduced motion.

        Motion: calm and alive
