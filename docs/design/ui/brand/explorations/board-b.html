B.Light-first · a riso print studio

Nothing goes out until you sign it.

Your studio of agents, run like a print shop. Every step is laid out on a proof you can read, every job carries its slip with the cost, and one colour on screen is yours: the proofing pink that means your call.

01The concept

The Proof

"Signed by you." A studio that prints its work where you can read it, and runs nothing until you sign.

The glass box
In print, nothing goes to press until the client signs the proof. Bench's promise is the same rule: every step, prompt and token is on the proof, and outward actions wait for your signature.
The team
A studio of specialists, each with their own spot ink. Many inks on one sheet still read as one publication: one team.
The cost
Every print job carries a docket slip: time, stock, run size. Every Bench job carries its slip too: minutes, % of your week, files touched, who gets notified.
Your call
Proofreaders mark in one colour. Here that colour is pink, and it appears once per screen, on the decision that is yours.
Candidnot clinical

Prints the numbers on the slip. Says what it did and couldn't do.

Craftednot precious

Paper, ink, crop marks: made by hand, used every day.

Warmnot cute

Characters with eyes, printed slightly imperfect. No jokes, no emoji.

Exactnot technical

Exact times and counts. The machinery stays on the proof, one click away.

Deferentialnot passive

You're the client and the editor. The studio drafts, proofs and asks; it never runs outward on its own.

02Mark, wordmark, icon

An ink block that looks back, printed off-register

The mark is a character from the studio's own family: a black ink block with paper eyes, and the pink proof layer peeking out underneath. It survives any rename: the name is a slot, the block is the brand.

bench
Primary lockup

Mark + name in Schibsted Grotesk 800, lowercase, −0.05em, with a pink proof shadow offset by 0.04em. Above 32 px only.

bench
On ink

The block turns to paper, the eyes to ink. The pink layer never changes: it's the sign-off.

Sizes

At 16 px the pink layer and crop marks drop (they turn to mud); the block and eyes stay. The taskbar icon reads as a face at every size.

Rename grammar

[mark] + gap 0.3 × mark height + [name, 3–8 lowercase letters] at 0.72 × mark height, baselines on the block's lower third. The brand vocabulary (proof, slip, studio, register) never uses the name, so a rename touches one slot.

Placeholders "kin" and "workroom" only test widths; they are not name proposals.

Never

A gradient, a glow, an agent ink on the block, the pink as the block colour, an outline-only mark.

The offset is fixed

Pink sits 6/64 right and 6/64 down. It is the only place pink appears outside a decision: the mark says "you sign".

Clear space

One eye height around the lockup. Crop marks belong to the app icon only.

07Motion personality

Printed, not animated

Things move the way paper and ink do: sheets feed in, slips slide, stamps land, ink settles into register. Short, weighted, a touch mechanical. With reduced motion, each one rests on its final frame and the words still say it.
