# Round 6: away from the Euca wordmark

Method from [LESSONS.md](LESSONS.md): voice first, nouns, 20 ideas in 20 minutes, ownability check, black first, one stretch.

## Voice (the emotional temperature)
- **Five words Eucas would say:** held, seen, done, yours, draft.
- **Five it never would:** magic, awesome, autopilot, instantly, crush.
- **One post:** "Your half-built idea, seen through. The team's on it, and nothing sends until you've read it."
- **Temperature:** calm, warm, precise. Rounded but built; no sparkle, no shout.

## Nouns
Team · crew · eyes · glass · window · workshop · bench · story · ending · turn · draft · thread · needle · handoff · arch · stone · kite · string · light · lantern.

## Category to avoid (ownability gap)
Sparkles and asterisks (also too close to Claude's own mark), knots, orbs, robot faces, hexagons, chat bubbles, neural dots, gradient blobs.

## 20 ideas, one line each
1. A lantern whose glass shows the team inside.
2. A lit window at night (tried in v1, retired).
3. A lens with a pupil.
4. A full stop that smiles: the ending.
5. A bookmark whose notch is a smile: your place kept.
6. An open door spilling light.
7. A wave that pauses: the catch of breath.
8. Two cupped hands passing a dot: the handoff.
9. A relay baton.
10. A glass jar of fireflies: a team you can see, working at night.
11. A constellation that draws an e.
12. A paper plane (wrong: agents never send).
13. **A kite: your idea flies, and you keep the string.**
14. A ship in a bottle: finished craft behind glass.
15. A bench seen side-on: the codename.
16. **A keystone arch: every stone holds once the last one is set.**
17. Dawn through a window.
18. A ribbon folded into an e: a page turning.
19. **A dovetail joint: pieces made to fit, from the workshop.**
20. **A thread through the eye of a needle that ties into an e: your idea, seen through.**

## Shortlist
- **A · Keystone** (abstract): the team is the stones; the last one, set, makes the arch stand. *The ending that holds.*
- **B · Kite** (pictorial-abstract): four panels lifted together on one string that ends with you. *Human in the loop, drawn.*
- **C · Dovetail** (abstract): two pieces cut to fit, no glue. *Handoffs that fit.*
- **D · Seen through** (stretch): a thread passes through a needle's eye and loops into an e. *A pun on the brand line.*
