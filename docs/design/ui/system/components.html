Components

Every core component in every state it can be in, built only from tokens.css. Dark first, like the app; switch to Light at the right. Forced states are rendered side by side; the live ones also respond to your pointer and keyboard. New in v0.6 marks what the prototype added since v1.1.

Theme

Colour

60 % ground (neutral Charcoal on dark, Porcelain on light), 30 % surfaces and Ink text, 10 % meaning: one Cobalt action per screen, amber for what needs you, agent hues on their own things. Values and ratios are read live from the tokens for the theme that’s on.

Agent hues

Ten identities. Base for the avatar body, light and deep for the hero render, ink for name tags and mentions, tint for your bubbles in that agent’s chat, wash for its canvas on light (on dark the wash is the plain charcoal ground). Never on a button, never a status.

Type

Instrument Sans in two weights, six steps far apart; JetBrains Mono for code, commands and the trace. Both SIL OFL, bundled with the app. A single view uses four steps at most; the h1 is the only headline.

Space, shape and depth

A 4-pt scale used by relationship: 8 inside a group, 16 between groups, 32 between sections. One continuous-corner family. Elevation is tone first; only floating things cast a shadow.

Name and face New in v0.6

An agent’s face and name are one door to its profile and settings, Atlas’s too. The chevron says it opens; hovering the face lights the pill, so the two read as one control. The face is a pointer target only; the pill is the tab stop.

One way in everywhere: the face, the name, or the last breadcrumb all open the same panel on Settings › Profile. A long name ellipsizes inside the pill; the pill never shrinks below its name’s first letters.

Messages

Your bubbles take the agent’s tint; the agent’s sit on the surface over its wash (plain charcoal on dark). Every agent message carries its cost and a way to look inside. System lines are centred and quiet.

@-mention picker New in v0.6.1

Every chat, agent messages and your own. Reply to the whole message (its button, R, or right-click), or select words and reply to just those. The preview joins the composer; sent, the quote leads back to the original.

Blurred scroll edge New in v0.6.1

Older messages don’t stop at a hard line under a sticky hero or header. They soften into a short progressive blur and fade into the canvas. Scroll each sample.

Before (v0.6): a 16 px fade, so lines look cut off mid-word.
v0.6.1: a 40 px strip, blur 6 px at the edge to 0 at its foot, over a fade to the wash.
Reduced transparency: the same edge as a plain fade, no blur.

One strip per scroller, never a full-area blur: backdrop-filter: blur(var(--edge-blur)) on a var(--edge-h) pseudo-element with a mask-image from opaque to transparent, over a gradient of the canvas colour. It appears only while something is under the edge (scrollTop > 0). Works on both themes because the canvas colour is a role token.

Side-panel rows

The selected row is a neutral pill, not a colour. Live work replaces the snippet; what needs you turns the snippet amber and adds a count. Collapses to an avatar rail.

Row anatomy

56 px, two lines: avatar 32 with badge · name 15/600 · time 12 · snippet 13 in Text 2. Rows with a subline never go below a 44 px pitch.

States in order on the left: default, hover, selected, focus. On the right: working (typing), needs you, blocked, paused, and a long name truncated with an ellipsis.

Group headers carry their actions as 24 px icon buttons (Dashboard, Team chat, New) and a visible “…” menu; the same menu opens on right-click.

Agent panel New in v0.6

Closed by default; the screen icon opens it. It starts with a compact live preview of the agent’s screen (click to enter the full view), the context meter, then routines as compact rows: name, when, and the cost per run. A row opens to its figures.

Anatomy

Preview: a 128 px thumbnail of the live diff (7 px mono, add and delete tints from the diff tokens), then the file, its +/− and the live word with a dot in the agent’s ink. Hover darkens the ring. With nothing on screen it is one quiet line, not an empty box.

Context: “Context”, a 4 px bar with your checkpoint tick and the hatched 90 % ceiling, the percent in 600. In the chat it joins the top-right tools only from 60 %.

Routine rows: name 13/600, when and ≈ cost a run in 12 Text 3, a switch on the right. Hover and open share the ground fill; an off routine’s name drops to Text 2. Left: default with one row hovered. Right: one row open, one off.

Project and routine cards

Project cards answer “how far along, and who needs me” in that order. Routine cards show the schedule in words and three figures: next run, last run with its cost, the estimate.

Approval card

One pattern wherever a request appears: the inbox, the workspace pane and the ticket. The consequence comes before the buttons, and allowing it in one place clears it everywhere.

Rules

Items are 32 px; inner radius is the menu’s minus its padding. Hover and keyboard focus share one fill; focus adds the ring. A disabled item says why on its right instead of a shortcut. The menu opens from the pointer or the button, scaled from 0.97 over 200 ms, and closes on Esc with focus returned.

Empty, loading and error

Every empty state has a headline, a reason and one next step. Loading keeps the final layout with flat bars, no shimmer. An error stays inside the block that failed.
