Margin folk

Every agent is a doodle from the margin of your notebook that woke up and took a job: a star, a mug, a little TV, a frog, a sock. One soft matte shape, a tiny Ink face, noodle arms that reach for real things, and a topper you can spot across the office. Built from parts and moved by code, so agent number 13 costs nothing, and every move it makes comes from a real event.

The family

Four rules make them one family

The forms change; these four rules never do. That's what lets a frog and a mug sit at the same desk row and still read as one team, and what keeps them clear of Grok Bot's eye strokes and Dots' plush.

Behaviour = real state

Every state, on two creatures

Noor (coral mug, round eyes, steam) and Omar (teal TV, ring eyes, antenna). Each state is a preset of the same parameters; temperament comes from the agent's id, so the two never move in step. Blinks and topper flicks fire on events (a tool call, a turn in a debate), not on a timer.

Marks float above the topper at a fixed screen size in the office (24 px), so they read at any zoom. Escalation (stand, wave, hop, come forward) is capped at 3 agents at once; only the top Blocked need may come forward. Never a sound by default.

One design, two renders

Sizes: flat when small, soft clay when big

At 32 px and below the creature is a flat bust: body, topper and face, no limbs, eyes grown to stay at least 1.3 px. From 40 px it gets the full body and the soft render: one radial light, a matte falloff, bottom occlusion and a contact shadow. No gloss, no fur.

Hover a topper to flick it: one damped spring (stiffness 180, damping 12) drives every topper, so a sprout sways, an antenna boings and steam keeps rising.

The library

14 forms × 11 toppers

Every combination works; hue and eyes rotate here only to show range. Bean, drop and pebble were left out on purpose: they are Grok Bot's droplet, egg and blob. Uniqueness on a floor is form + colour (UX creature-picker §6).

Create your agent

Atlas suggests, you keep it or play

The look row of New agent, expanded. Atlas's suggestion is already applied, so you can press Create without opening it. Every option has its name; taken colours on this floor show who has them. Try it: each change makes the creature blink and flick its topper.

Everything else stays

Creatures in the existing components

Only the avatar changes. Rows, chat, mentions, tickets, the badge and the words are the design system's, untouched: the status is always a badge and a word, never the creature alone.

For the 3D build

The rig contract

One parameter vector for every creature. States are presets of it; transitions interpolate; the 3D rig and this SVG take the same values. Details and per-state values in CREATURES.md.
