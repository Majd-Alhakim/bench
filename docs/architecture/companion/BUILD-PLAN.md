# Build plan: the Eucas companion v0.1

**Scope (updated 2026-10-26):**
- **Base:** the pre-mortem's cut-down v0.1 (SYSTEM-DESIGN §1). Pro is a waitlist button: no payments, no per-session budgets, no cloud hand-off.
- **Added by CR-010 (founder, 2026-10-26):** choosing and locking your rules in setup (A-2), prompted rules (R-2) and the plain-code guided merge (M-1), in v0.1's Free tier. v0.1 ships **without Authenticode signing** (ADR-0013).
- **Added by CR-011 and the founder's decisions of 2026-10-26:**
  - your Euca in **the native notch** with **one panel**; no main window (ADR-0016);
  - the **supervisor meter**;
  - **the supervisor itself, always on for every user**: a 14-day trial ~~from the end of setup~~ ~~(start point open, recommended at sign-up)~~ from account creation (founder, 2026-10-26, decided), then a monthly taste; Pro's hidden budget is about $1.48; SP-SUP2 is the gate (ADR-0011, ADR-0018).
- **Added by CR-012 and the founder's decisions:**
  - **Eucas accounts** (email + code; ~~required to finish setup~~ offered, not required, since the no-account revision below; since 2026-10-26 asked after the four limits are locked, so protection never waits for it);
  - **per-account local profiles** (a new account starts from zero);
  - **refusing disposable email domains** (ADR-0017).
- **Added by the no-account revision (founder, 2026-10-26: *"we make people have a small taste without sign up and then they have to create account or buy pro"*, then *"Let the no account also have to try the supervisor also but like small number of usage"*, then the answers: *"Everything a free account sees but with limited supervisour usage and no memory and 1 custom role"*):** setup can finish without an account; no account = **everything Free sees, minus memory** (no learns-you, What Pip knows or learned suggestions, including the rule suggestions from repeated allows), **one rule of your own**, and a **supervisor taste of 10 decisions per install** (an anonymous install token; drafting uses it; ADR-0012, ADR-0018); one account-state gate in the core (SYSTEM-DESIGN §4.2). The trial starts at account creation. Stories AS-1, AN-1, NA-1, NA-2 and Δ on O-1, SUP-1, R-2, AC-1 (**+4.5 days**, §3.1; was +5.25 before the founder answers).
- **Changed by the spikes** (SP-H1, SP-ST1, SP-TW1): stories G-3, G-4, T-1, U-1 and C-1.
- **Changed by the founder (BRD §10 #60):** the guided merge follows the approved design (M-1).

**Estimates** are focused build days for the founder working with Claude (one Claude developer lane, founder reviewing), from the pre-mortem's per-item figures adjusted for this design. They are estimates, not commitments; the day-6 re-plan (CR-009 practice) corrects them.

**The new total: about 52.75 build days** (was 48.25 before the no-account revision of 2026-10-26; 32 before CR-011), plus **about 0.75 spike days** still to run before Gate 6 and **SP-SUP2** (2 days + labelling) running alongside the build as the supervisor's launch gate. Where the days come from is in §3.1.
