## 7. Things tried that failed
- **The safety check blocks recursive deletes, `git worktree remove` and globbed `rm` after a `cd`.** Write to new folders instead, and leave deletes to the client.
- **Headless Edge in a bash loop wrote nothing** (exit 127 or 0), because of a `\\$var` path quirk in MSYS. Fix: `cygpath -m` paths, or puppeteer-core with Edge (`scripts/capture.mjs`).
- **Long Python heredocs with triple quotes broke bash** ("unexpected EOF"). Write the script to the scratchpad and run it.
- **Gemini video watching hit 503/504 under load**, even with four running in parallel. Fix: retry one at a time, or use the local engine (`yt-dlp` installed through winget; it reads the captions and frames).
- **Removing items from `NEEDS0` in the prototype threw an error** (line 4753 edits Yusuf's item). Fix: hide them with `H.done.add(k)` instead.
- **Remotion `interpolate` throws on Infinity ranges.** Guard the last chapter.

## 8. Mistakes and their fixes
- **Six logo rounds went generic before the client steered.** Fix: research the category first (the videos' method: voice, nouns, 20 ideas, ownability), and ask early whether to refine or replace.
- **The video's first app scene was a fake mock-up, and it was too fast to follow.** Fix: use the real screens, one chapter per step, with captions and title labels.
- **The first cut's transitions were dissolves between screens at different zoom levels.** Fix: cut on the beat with a camera settle, and scroll in place for chat updates.
