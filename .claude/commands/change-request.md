description: Handle a change to an approved (baselined) requirement or design, with an impact analysis
argument-hint: "<what the client wants to change>"
---

Change: $ARGUMENTS

1. Create `docs/requirements/change-requests/CR-NNN.md` from `docs/templates/CHANGE-REQUEST.md`.
2. Analyse the impact with the BA (use cases), the tech lead (effort and risk) and the designers (screens): which IDs, stories and sprints are affected.
3. Present the options with a recommendation and record the client's decision. If approved, bump the affected document versions, update the stories, and log it in STATUS.md.
