---
description: End of sprint. Demo, client acceptance per story, metrics, and retrospective
---

1. For each committed story, show the evidence (PR, test results, QA verdict, screenshots or a build) and ask the client to accept or reject it. Rejected stories go back to the backlog with the reason.
2. Record the metrics in the sprint README: velocity, bugs found and fixed, tokens used.
3. Retrospective: what went well, what to improve, and at most 2 action items, each with an owner. Apply the process improvements to the docs, commands or agents in a `chore/` PR.
4. Update STATUS.md and propose the next `/plan-sprint`.
