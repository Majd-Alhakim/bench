description: Phase 6/7. Plan the next sprint (goal, stories, tasks, owners) for client approval
---

Precondition: G6 is approved. For Sprint 1, G3, G4 and G5 must be approved and the backlog must exist.

1. Read the backlog (GitHub issues labelled `story`, or `docs/BACKLOG.md`) and the action items from the last sprint's retro in `docs/sprints/`.
2. The `product-manager` proposes a sprint goal and stories that meet the Definition of Ready, within capacity.
3. The `tech-lead` splits each story into tasks (files, tests, dependencies) and puts tasks that touch the same files in sequence.
4. Write `docs/sprints/sprint-NN/README.md` from `docs/templates/SPRINT.md` and present it to the client for approval.
5. Once approved, set the stories to `Ready` on the board and assign one `developer` agent per task.
